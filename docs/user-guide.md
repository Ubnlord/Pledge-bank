# PLEDGE BANK website guide

## Public website
The landing page introduces PLEDGE BANK and links to the About, Services, Contact, Login, and Registration preview pages.

## Login and registration
These pages are visual previews only. They do not create accounts, authenticate users, send verification emails, or store submitted form information. Do not enter real passwords or personal financial information.

## Customer dashboard
`src/dashboard/overview.html` is a concept page, not a protected dashboard. Real customer pages must be served only after verified authentication and server/database authorization.

## Before banking services launch
Customers must receive accurate information about the licensed service provider, eligibility, fees, privacy, customer support, and applicable protections. No deposit, transfer, card, loan, or payment service is active in this repository yet.
