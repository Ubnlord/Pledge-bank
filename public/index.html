Helpful answers

Questions, answered.

Here are a few useful things to know before you get started.

Which currencies does PLEDGE BANK focus on?

The current account experience is designed around US dollars (USD) and euros (EUR). It does not offer local-currency wallets in this version.

What if I sign in but don’t see an account balance?

A successful sign-in does not automatically mean an account has been assigned. If your dashboard says no account is assigned, contact support. We never fill the screen with invented balances.

Can I make transfers, deposits or buy Bitcoin right now?

Those money-movement and purchase features are not active in this version. Do not send funds or cryptocurrency based on this website until an official, verified workflow is available.

How can I protect my account?

Use a unique password, keep one-time codes private, and never share your password with anyone claiming to be support. Use the official contact page if you need help.
